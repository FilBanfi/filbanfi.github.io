EcoPoints

Recognize different types of waste by scanning them with your camera, using an on-device AI photo recognition model. EcoPoints was one of the first free and open-source projects of this kind in Italy.

⚠️ Project Discontinued
This project is no longer actively maintained and will not receive further updates. However, it remains fully open source: feel free to fork the repository, update the AI model, and continue its development!
View Source Code Download APK

Features

On-device AI

All image recognition happens locally on your device. No data is sent to the cloud.

Free and Open Source

Transparent codebase, built for the community and fully auditable.

Accessibility Friendly

Designed with simplicity in mind to be easy to use for everyone.

Italian Language Only

Currently, the app interface and AI responses are available exclusively in Italian.

How to Use

  1. Grant Permissions: Allow camera access when prompted by the app.
  2. Scan: Point your camera at the waste item and press the scan button.
  3. Enjoy: Get instant feedback on how to properly sort your garbage and earn points!

Development History & Roadmap

Note: The official detailed timeline and historical versions have been lost, but the development journey was approximately as follows:

Phase 1: Foundation

Initial Shell & Google AI

Built the initial app structure and integrated the first Google AI recognition model.

Phase 2: Iteration

Microsoft Model & Kodular

Experimented with a Microsoft AI model and briefly moved development to the Kodular platform.

Phase 3: Stabilization

Return to MIT App Inventor

Moved back to MIT App Inventor due to stability issues. Implemented the current MIT AI model, added detailed recycling instructions, and rebalanced the scoring system.

Phase 4: Open for Forks

Community Driven

Active development is halted. The source code is available for anyone to download, update the AI model, and continue the project.
